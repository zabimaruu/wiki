# FlashForge 5M Guide & Maintanance

## Must Do Regurarely

### Threaded Rods Noise
There could be times when the `bed` goes up and down, the threaded rods might make a squeaking noise. This is due to poor lubrication

Apply a decent amount of [Lithium Grease](https://www.amazon.com/Lucas-Oil-10533-Lithium-Grease/dp/B004X70LZA?crid=2FN4YGW7SH3JO&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.fLuQDol9bz0IcIaCp2DpSjHKJ1PPYySJMUUeGraxfVSJGoOmp9Xpxbll0rqHaVBQT9zm-ea5Z87AJgnTv5t299uGD_FvWZCsoC-93UnzY8KTH3GM1xRsEjhgSKlt8sAMNsgsda27yLPjhIELLYAHiRNDRjqzSFs6bybdxIM8Apie3thJZmbg0x1fdmzNkIimZPToLramsnTc1Zou9cEBRPrfK4_4uzYWleyGB7DPrK1gKdPdP9vX8B-2DgqlprZt4v1b32OoiSztmAs2z0lGcBiUNfRosa72L1Qp8nDQWCo.dXo5RJ5ipcyfHeMWJ175SI15Ei7177Sdl5SEt4_rKh4&dib_tag=se&keywords=Lithium%2BGrease&qid=1769825998&sprefix=%2Caps%2C327&sr=8-6&th=1)
